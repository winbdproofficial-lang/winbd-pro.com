🔑 Tips for Playing Responsibly

Here are some simple strategies to keep play safe and fun:

  • ⏳ Limit Your Time: Take breaks while playing.

  • 📱 Use App Tools: Use reminders and settings to help manage your play.
  • 🌟 Play for Fun: Treat games as entertainment, not guaranteed income.
